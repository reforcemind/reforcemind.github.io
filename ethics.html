Alignment Specification

// constraints & friction

Alignment in highly scaled Vision-Language-Action (VLA) architectures cannot be achieved through post-hoc filtering. It requires parameterizing behavioral friction directly into the objective function during the pre-training and post-training phases.

Mitigation of Reward Hacking

Classical reinforcement learning often converges on proxy-reward hacking when environments lack fidelity. To prevent value-misalignment, we utilize multi-agent adversarial environments. Secondary agents act as constraint verifiers, introducing dynamic penalties for policy collapse or proxy gaming.

  • Verification policies run continuously in parallel to the main training loop.
  • Penalty gradients are injected dynamically, forcing robust generalization.

Out-of-Distribution Safety Bounds

When VLA models encounter OOD (Out-of-Distribution) states, action certainty drops. Rather than defaulting to hallucinated outputs, our framework enforces a hard structural fallback. Latent representations exhibiting entropy above predefined thresholds trigger a systemic halt or conservative failure mode.

Conservative Failure Architecture

By restricting the continuous action space strictly to known, verified sub-spaces during high-entropy events, the model avoids catastrophic misprediction in physical deployment scenarios.

Parameterizing Friction

We do not define "ethics" as an abstract moral concept, but as mathematical friction applied to unsafe state transitions. This reduces complex philosophical alignment problems into quantifiable gradient descent objectives.
